Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for services provided by Assembloid Space (Business Registration No. 57978800-000-05-25-2, registered in Hong Kong). It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Bespoke professional services

Our engagements are bespoke research and consultancy services scoped individually for each client. Fees, the payment schedule, and any engagement-specific cancellation terms are set out in the written proposal or statement of work ("Proposal") you approve before work begins. Where a Proposal sets out different terms, those terms take precedence over this policy for that engagement.

2. Cancellation before work begins

You may cancel an engagement at any time before work has commenced by emailing jennleungx@gmail.com. If no work has been carried out and no non-recoverable third-party costs have been incurred, any deposit or advance payment you have made will be refunded in full.

3. Cancellation after work has begun

If you cancel after work has begun, you will be charged for work performed and for any non-recoverable costs committed on your behalf up to the date we receive your written cancellation. Where you have pre-paid more than this amount, we will refund the difference. Where the work performed exceeds amounts already paid, we will invoice you for the balance.

4. Deposits

Deposits reserve capacity and cover initial scoping and preparation. Deposits are refundable in full only where cancellation occurs before work begins (see section 2). Once work has begun, deposits are applied against work performed as described in section 3.

5. Milestones & completed deliverables

Fees for milestones or deliverables that have been completed and delivered are non-refundable. This does not affect your rights in respect of services that are not performed with reasonable skill and care (see section 6).

6. Services not performed as agreed

If you believe a deliverable has not been provided with reasonable skill and care or does not meet the agreed Proposal, contact us within 14 days of delivery. We will work with you in good faith to correct or re-perform the affected work at no additional cost. Where we are unable to remedy the issue, we will provide a fair refund for the affected portion of the services.

7. How to request a cancellation or refund

Send cancellation and refund requests to jennleungx@gmail.com, including your name, the engagement or invoice reference, and the reason for your request. We aim to acknowledge requests within 5 business days.

8. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible, and are normally processed within 14 business days of approval. Timing of receipt may depend on your bank or card issuer.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jennleungx@gmail.com.
